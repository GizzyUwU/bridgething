## Highlights
- OTA transfers ack on a rolling byte window instead of only at chunk boundaries, so streams keep flowing on slow or lossy links.
- update progress only redraws when the percent actually moves, ending the stall-then-jump reporting during long transfers.
- companion OTA streaming reworked on both platforms for cleaner resume and fewer stuck updates.
- builtin hub and stock webapps now ride the daemon release and update over OTA.
